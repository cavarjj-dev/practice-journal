Stop signals & help

Stop the session now

Heart racing or panic · the image feels literally real · a combat memory or flashback · numbness, “not here” · an urge to self-harm · the figure demands you act on something in outer life right now.

Open your eyes, do the grounding routine below, close the book. No after-reflection today.

Pause the practice 1–2 weeks

Two nights of worse sleep · more irritability with family or clients · images intruding during the day · more worry, not less · noticeable cognitive fatigue the next day.

Keep ordinary routines; tell your therapist or a trusted person.

Get professional help

Signals persist after a pause · nightmares increase · feeling special, chosen, or on a mission from the material · losing track of time · any suicidal thoughts.

Contact your VA provider or a therapist.

Grounding routine (5 minutes)

  1. Eyes open. Feet flat on the floor. Press down for 10 seconds.
  2. Breathe out longer than in: in 4, out 6, five rounds.
  3. 5-4-3-2-1: name 5 things you see, 4 you hear, 3 you can touch, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.
  4. Orient out loud: today’s day and date, where you are, your name, what you’ll do next.
  5. Stand, drink water, walk 2–5 minutes, outside if possible.
  6. Close the book. Put the journal and the Red Book away.
